Frequently asked questions

How much is delivery?

Delivery starts at 20 MAD. The exact price depends on distance; we confirm it on WhatsApp before leaving.

Do you deliver at night?

Yes, we deliver 24/7, including nights and weekends, from the open on-duty pharmacy.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery: the medicine price (pharmacy receipt) plus delivery.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes for prescription medicines. For other products (baby care, para-pharmacy…) just send the name.

Which areas?

All of Tangier: Malabata, Branes, Iberia, Californie, Najma, Jbel Kbir, Mesnana, Beni Makada and every other area.
